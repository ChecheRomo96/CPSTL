Detailed Description

A subset of <algorithm>: min, max, copy, copy_backward, fill, equal, find, iter_swap and sort.

In STL mode these are the std functions. Otherwise sort is a heapsort: O(n log n) in the worst case, no recursion and no extra memory, which suits small stacks; like std::sort it is not stable.
